# Porto Rocha Design System

## Overview

Porto Rocha describes itself as: “PORTO ROCHA is a global strategy and design agency with offices in New York and London. We help brands navigate complex challenges through work that is strategically rigorous, emotionally resonant, and beautifully design” The interface is light, built on **White (#FFFFFF)** with **Near Black (#000000)** text and almost no color: actions are carried by contrast, not hue.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use #FFFFFF for the page and lift surfaces in small steps: #F5F5F5.
- Do let contrast carry emphasis; primary actions use #000000 on #FFFFFF.
- Do keep spacing on the 8px grid.
- Do use 8px corners on most elements; larger surfaces also use 10px.
- Don't add drop shadows for depth; use tonal surfaces.
- Don't introduce new font families beyond sf-pro-display and sf-pro-text.
- Don't add saturated accent colors; the measured palette is almost entirely neutral.
- Don't make interface transitions slower than 0.5s.
